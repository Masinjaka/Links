# Privacy Policy for Rohy

**Effective date: July 31, 2026**

Rohy is a mobile application for saving and organizing links. This policy
explains how Rohy handles information when you use the app.

## Information Rohy handles

Rohy may store the following information on your device:

- Links you save or share with the app
- Titles, descriptions, notes, tags, and collections you create
- Webpage metadata, such as preview images, icons, and reading time
- App preferences, including appearance and language settings

Rohy does not require an account. The app does not include advertising,
analytics, tracking, or a Rohy-operated server that receives your library.

## How information is used

Your information is used only to provide app features, including saving,
organizing, searching, previewing, exporting, and sharing links.

## Network requests and third parties

When you save a link, Rohy may connect directly to that website to retrieve its
title, description, icon, and preview image. The website and its service
providers may receive standard network information, such as your IP address and
the requested URL, under their own privacy policies.

If you open a link, share content, export a CSV file, or use another app or
system service, that service may handle the information according to its own
privacy policy. Apple, Google, and your device platform may also process app
store, installation, diagnostic, or backup information independently of Rohy.

Rohy does not sell your personal information.

## Storage and retention

Your Rohy library is stored locally on your device and remains there until you
delete it or remove the app. Device or cloud backups managed by your operating
system may retain a copy according to your backup settings.

Information you export or share is stored wherever you choose to send
it. Rohy cannot control or delete copies held by other apps or services.

## Your choices

You can:

- Edit or delete individual links, tags, and collections
- Erase your saved library data from Rohy's settings
- Export your links to CSV
- Remove locally stored app data by uninstalling Rohy

## Children's privacy

Rohy is not designed to collect personal information from children, and the
developer does not knowingly collect such information through the app.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Rohy's features or privacy practices change.
The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the contact
information provided on Rohy's app store listing.
